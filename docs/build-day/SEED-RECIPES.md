## seed-01｜Photo + Geometric Editorial Poster

- **效果摘要：** 照片上半部保留、下半部轉為幾何模組插畫；3:4，嚴格對半；最終圖不得有任何文字。

- **Prompt 在平台收錄與執行的依據：** 待確認
- **測試圖片／實測輸出／使用的生成服務：** 待補；目前沒有聲稱已測試

### Prompt 原文（由使用者提供，未改寫）

~~~text
Create a separate, high-end design poster for each photo I’ve uploaded—do not combine multiple images; output each photo individually. Use a **3:4 portrait composition overall**, with the top and bottom sections strictly maintaining a **1:1 height ratio**, each occupying 50% of the frame.
The **upper half** should retain the original photograph, preserving the subject’s identity, structure, pose, authentic texture, natural lighting and shadows, and original color palette. Apply only subtle, sophisticated color grading to give it the aesthetic of an art magazine, independent publication, or contemporary exhibition image. To fit the aspect ratio, the background may be naturally expanded, but the subject must not be stretched, distorted, replaced, or altered.
The **lower half** extracts the most recognizable elements of the subject—including its contours, structure, posture, proportions, and narrative relationships—and reconstructs them into a **geometric, modular illustration**. Do not replicate the photograph in its entirety, nor depict it realistically. Instead, eliminate irrelevant details and retain only the visual elements that best represent the subject’s identity and character.
Reconstruct the image through **geometric abstraction, overlapping shapes, modular forms, and collage-style composition**, allowing viewers to instantly recognize the correspondence between the photograph above and the illustration below.
The overall design emphasizes **blocks, modules, geometric division, and large cohesive planes**. Geometric divisions should not be overly fragmented or cut into numerous small pieces. Instead, subjects should be organized using fewer, larger, and more complete **rectangles, squares, rounded squares, ellipses, triangles, circles, and irregular organic shapes**.
Preserve the essential visual characteristics of the original subject while translating them into a sophisticated graphic language. Figures may be rendered with square faces, distinct jawlines, and block-like facial features. Architecture may be transformed into square volumes, facade divisions, rhythmic openings, and structural transitions. Animals, plants, objects, food, and landscapes should similarly be distilled into bold geometric forms.
Use a **premium contemporary editorial aesthetic**, balancing photography and graphic abstraction. Maintain strong visual harmony between the upper photographic half and lower illustrated half through composition, proportions, shapes, textures, and color relationships.
**Absolutely no text, typography, letters, words, numbers, captions, slogans, logos, labels, signatures, or graphic lettering anywhere in the final poster.** The design must communicate entirely through imagery, geometric forms, composition, color, and visual storytelling.
~~~

### Prompt 原文（由使用者提供，未改寫）

~~~text
Please create one standalone editorial poster for each input photo. Do not make a collage. Output each photo separately. Use a vertical 3:4 composition, strictly divided into upper and lower halves, each occupying 50% of the frame.

Upper half: Keep the original photograph. Preserve the subject unchanged, along with its real texture, natural lighting, and original color mood. Apply only subtle editorial color grading to give the image the quality of an art magazine, independent publication, or exhibition photography. To fit the composition, you may slightly extend the sky, ground, or surrounding background, but do not stretch, distort, or alter the subject.

Lower half: Reinterpret the most recognizable subject, silhouette, pose, and spatial relationship from the photo into a minimalist layered paper-cut / paper-art composition. Use one main subject as the visual anchor. Do not mechanically copy every detail. Instead, reconstruct the key contours, proportions, and structure through folding, cutting, layering, and masking. Convert a few supporting environmental elements into foreground, middle-ground, and background paper layers. Use scale contrast, negative space, clean alignment, and overlapping depth so the subject remains instantly recognizable, while maintaining a restrained editorial composition.

Color palette: Extract the most distinctive and recognizable colors from the original image, then reduce them to a limited and harmonious paper-art palette. Use warm ivory white or light paper as the base, supported by a small amount of a clearer accent color, a darker structural color, soft layered transition colors, and very few highlight colors. The material should show high-quality matte cardstock texture, with visible fine paper fibers, clean cut edges, and soft cast shadows under natural diffused light. Emphasize the tactile quality of real handmade work. Avoid glossy plastic-like 3D effects, cheap craft paper texture, neon colors, and overly saturated complex gradients.

Typography system: Add a refined editorial typography system. Create a short main title based on the subject’s identity, location, action, material, mood, or symbolic meaning. Add 2 to 4 smaller supporting text elements, which may include the object name, location information, numbers, sequence markers, coordinate-like numbers, directional words, status words, material words, category labels, or a very short poetic phrase. Do not use years. The main title should carry emotion and identity, while the smaller texts add rhythm, order, and refinement.

Integrate the text naturally into the composition so it interacts with the paper-cut forms. Place text along paper edges, subject contours, geometric axes, or negative spaces. You may use horizontal layout, vertical layout, rotated typography, wide letter spacing, edge alignment, corner placement, cross-block layout, embedded text, or overlapping masking. The main title may have a subtle paper-cut or origami feeling, while the small text should remain thin, modern, and restrained. The final result should be elegant, tactile, balanced, and distinctly editorial in feeling.
~~~

## 展示前先補的資料

1. 逐一開原始貼文，確認貼文、prompt 與展示圖確實對應；記下發文者名稱和帳號。Threads 分享短網址目前無法在此直接核對，不能猜作者。
2. 找到並核對原作者 IG；若沒有，就保留「查看原始貼文」，不要填別人的 IG。
3. 取得可展示的原作預覽圖，記錄圖檔來源與可在 prototype 使用的依據。沒有圖就先用文字卡與原貼文連結。
4. 確認 prompt 可以在平台收錄及代入試用的依據，與預覽圖分開記錄。
5. 準備一張可上傳的測試照片，實際跑過至少一份 prompt；保留生成服務、日期、圖片與結果，方便 Build Day fallback 誠實展示。

**優先順序：** 先把一份 recipe 補齊、跑通，再補第二、三份。後三格只在時間和素材允許時填；不需要為了湊滿六張卡而編造資料。
